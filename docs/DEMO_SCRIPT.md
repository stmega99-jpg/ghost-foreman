# Demo video script (2:50, English subtitles)

**The story in one line:** adding AIs was supposed to give you time back, but every AI keeps stopping to ask you something. Ghost Foreman takes over that supervising, so you only make the decisions that are really yours, from wherever you are.

**Who it is for:** anyone who works with several AIs at once: solo developers, founders, writers, researchers, and people who don't write code. Nothing in it is specific to one field, and the video should not look like a tool for one kind of work. Use everyday topics (a newsletter, a product launch, a research summary) for the agents' tasks and the meeting.

**Rules this video follows** (Kiroween 2026 Devpost): under 3 minutes (target 2:50), public on YouTube, the app shown **really running** on the PC and phone it runs on, no copyrighted music. English screen (language switch: English) so the picture matches the subtitles. No voice-over needed.

**Honesty rules** (the video must never break these):

- Every scene except the "public demo" shot is recorded on a **real Kiro Crew Gateway with real AIs**. The public demo shot is **sample data** and is labelled on screen as such.
- **A push notification reaching a real iPhone is not confirmed.** The phone scene opens the app by hand. Only if a push really arrives during recording may it be shown. Never write "notified" in a subtitle unless the push really arrived in that take.
- Speed-ups are labelled "sped up". Nothing is staged as if an AI said it.
- Not shown as working: voice control (not built), PC control (simulated), scheduled news (not built), conflict detection between AIs (not built).
- A scene that does not work during rehearsal is **cut**, and its claim goes too. Don't keep the subtitle without the picture.

### 2:10–2:30 An emergency stop and the final say

This is a **real stop of AIs that are really working**, recorded in one take. What it stops is exactly what **Ghost Foreman manages**: the Kiro Crew sessions it runs (its "slots") and the Claude Code / Codex programs it started for a meeting. Gemini gets no further request. AI sessions or apps you started yourself outside Ghost Foreman are not touched, and the subtitle says so.

## Before recording (checklist)

- [ ] Gateway running the current `main`. Emergency stop **not** in force. Screen language: English.
- [ ] **Tasks that are not about games.** Give three or four real AIs everyday tasks, for example: draft a short newsletter, write a launch checklist for a small online product, summarise a public article. Tell them to consult Ghost Foreman when unsure (the `foreman-protocol` skill and the `foreman_ask` tool).
- [ ] **Rules for the auto-answer.** Create one rule beforehand that matches the 0:15 question (for example "Use American spelling in all writing"). Make sure no rule covers the 0:25 question.
- [ ] **Rehearse the 0:48 shot.** After "Make this our policy from now on", check that the same kind of question is really answered automatically. If not, cut that shot.
- [ ] **Phone.** Ghost Foreman Home Screen app, signed in. Hide the address bar, the tailnet name, the device name, other apps' notifications, your e-mail and account names. Blur anything that slips in.
- [ ] **Meeting topic.** Something not private (the Gemini permission sends the meeting's text to a free tier).
- [ ] **Kiro shot.** A Kiro IDE window with no personal paths, user names or e-mail visible.
- [ ] **Stop shot (real AIs).** Right before 2:10, make sure at least one Ghost Foreman slot shows **Working**, then start a meeting with Claude Code and Codex and press Stop all **during round 1**, while they are running. Rehearse once: after the stop, the slots say Stopped, the meeting says it was cancelled, and the run record shows the runs ended by the emergency stop. If any of these does not show, fix the setup and record again rather than cutting around it. Lift the stop afterwards.
- [ ] **Public demo shot.** https://stmega99-jpg.github.io/ghost-foreman/ with the "Demo with sample data" banner in frame.
- [ ] Rehearse each scene once. Label every speed-up.
